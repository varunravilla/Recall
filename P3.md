# P3: Recall beta

A concert screenshot can be easy to find and still fail its owner: nothing brings
it back when tickets go on sale. Recall keeps the screenshot with a proposed
next step. Dated items can become reminders; useful items without dates stay in
Saved as **Found**. Recall supports events, places and food finds, and products.
It does not buy anything or make bookings.

## Journey: from concert screenshot to completed reminder

Maya saves a concert announcement because she wants to buy tickets when sales open.

1. She creates a password account and reaches **Inbox**. Returning users log in to
   the same account and saved collection.
2. In **Add photos**, she selects screenshots or an exported library folder,
   allows photo analysis, and chooses **Find my saves**. Recall checks the selected
   images without asking her to configure recognition or filtering.
3. The concert appears as **Ready to review**, with its stored image. **Review**
   opens the proposed type, action, and relevant time. Maya changes the action to
   “Buy concert tickets” and checks the sale time. **Save reminder** saves those
   displayed values and schedules the reminder.
4. **Saved** shows the item as Pending. Maya can open it and change the time while
   the reminder is scheduled. Reloading keeps the image, caption, and saved state.
5. When the time arrives, the open **Inbox** lists it under **Ready now**. After
   buying tickets elsewhere, Maya chooses **Mark complete**. The item remains in
   Saved as Completed and its reminder is cancelled. **No longer interested**
   dismisses the intention and also cancels its reminder.

An undated restaurant menu takes a shorter path. Gemini identifies the restaurant,
and Saved shows its image and caption as **Found**. No date is invented and no
reminder is created. An undated event is a saved idea, not a claim that it is upcoming.

Search and the All/To do/Done filters help Maya revisit her collection. **Delete**
opens a confirmation. **Keep item** cancels; **Delete item** removes the image,
interpretations, tracking history, and reminders. Another account cannot delete it.

## What changed since P2

| Earlier failure | Beta behavior |
| --- | --- |
| A name alone granted access | Password accounts, Argon2id hashes, and opaque 30-minute sessions |
| Photo import was simulated | Selected files/folders, automatic interpretation, and stored images |
| Every useful item needed a date | Undated finds remain useful without a fabricated reminder |
| Confirmation could ignore edits | Save reminder first stores the displayed proposal |
| Proposed items vanished from Saved | Collections remain visible without tracking or reminder records |
| An owned collection could accompany an unrelated interpretation | Mutations check ownership and the complete record relationship |
| Completion left reminders active | Successful completion/dismissal cancels the reminder |
| Refused deletion could trigger cleanup | Cleanup requires a successful removal response |

## Validation

The [saved validation output](docs/validation/README.md) records **60 passing tests,
zero failures, and 402 assertions**. Design checks, generated contracts, backend
and Vue type checks, and the production build pass. Tests cover concept actions,
refusals without unintended writes, record ownership, persistence, deletion
histories, quota failures, logout during inference, and date conversion. Ten-photo request and payload boundaries are covered.

Live Gemini checks verified a synthetic concert, stored image, duplicates,
and expired-event exclusion. The newest batch check sent two unique synthetic
images in one real request and returned one added item, one expired skip, and
one duplicate. Repeatable tests simulate provider responses. These checks do not establish
a complete browser or deployed journey. Earlier browser work covered registration,
Inbox, and the import layout; [remaining interface checks](docs/browser-checks.md)
are explicitly unverified.

## Browser scope and limits

The browser opens selected files or an exported folder; it cannot watch Apple
Photos or request native access to the whole library. Recognition can miss small, stylized, or unclear details. Gemini
can misread names and dates; check suggestions before scheduling a reminder.

Google receives photos only after consent and when analysis is allowed. Free-tier submissions may be used to improve
Google's products. [Import setup and privacy](docs/ai-import.md) explain the model,
quotas, file limits, storage, and retry behavior.

Imports send up to ten photos per request and prepare the next batch while AI
runs. Quota errors back off; Pause finishes the active batch and keeps waiting
photos. Leaving Add pauses the queue. A reload clears pending
files, but keeps accepted items. Login expires after 30 minutes. Reminders appear
in the open Inbox, not as background OS, email, or push notifications. Found items
can be kept or deleted; they have no reminder-editing or completion flow. Completed
items cannot be reopened, and password recovery is not implemented.
